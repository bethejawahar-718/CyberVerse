Privacy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What you enter

When you register, the name, email address and password you type are saved in your own browser's local storage, on your device. The password is stored as a salted hash, not as plain text. This information is not sent to any server run by CyberVerse.

What we do not use

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No advertising or third-party cookies.
  • No sharing or selling of data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and requested page). CyberVerse does not receive or use your account details from those logs.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored in your browser, you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Please use a throwaway password

This is an educational demo. Do not reuse a password from any real account.
