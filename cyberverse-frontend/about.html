About CyberVerse

A small educational project that outlines a beginner-friendly path into cybersecurity.

What it is

CyberVerse lays out a learning path covering cybersecurity fundamentals, security hygiene, common threats such as phishing and malware, defense basics, and practical labs.

Project status

This is a demo and work in progress. Labs, courses and AI mentors are planned but not built yet. Login and registration are a front-end demo: accounts are stored only in your own browser and nothing is sent to a server.
