04

Quick concepts

Use these reminders while exploring subnet sizes.

Network address

Identifies the subnet itself. In traditional subnets, it is not assigned to a regular host.

Broadcast address

The final address in a traditional IPv4 subnet, used to reach all hosts on that subnet.

CIDR prefix

The number after the slash tells how many of the 32 IPv4 bits belong to the network portion.

Host capacity

For most traditional IPv4 subnets, usable hosts equal total addresses minus two. /31 and /32 are special cases.
