01 / HALF ADDER

2 inputs · 2 outputs

A half adder adds input bits A and B. The SUM is their low bit, and CARRY is 1 when both inputs are 1.

02 / FULL ADDER

Add three bits

3 inputs · 2 outputs

A full adder adds A, B, and carry-in (Cin). It is useful when adding multi-bit binary numbers because it accepts a carry from the previous position.

FA
FULL ADDER
A, B, Cin → SUM + Cout
SUM0
CARRY OUT0
ABCinSUMCout

Formulas: SUM = A XOR B XOR Cin · Cout = (A AND B) OR (Cin AND (A XOR B))
