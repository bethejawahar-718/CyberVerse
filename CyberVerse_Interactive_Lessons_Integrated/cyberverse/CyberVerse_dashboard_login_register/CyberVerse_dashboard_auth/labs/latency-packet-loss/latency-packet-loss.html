03 / INTERPRETATION

What does it mean?

After a run, this area explains how your selected conditions may affect interactive applications.

Latency

How long data takes to travel and return. High latency makes interactions feel delayed.

Jitter

How much latency varies over time. High jitter can make voice or video choppy.

Packet loss

Packets that fail to arrive. Applications may need to recover, retransmit, or tolerate missing data.

Bandwidth

The available data-carrying capacity. High bandwidth does not automatically mean low latency.
