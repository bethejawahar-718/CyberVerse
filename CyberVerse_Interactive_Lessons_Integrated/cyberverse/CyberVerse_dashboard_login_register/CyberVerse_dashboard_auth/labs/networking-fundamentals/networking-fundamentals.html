LEARN FIRST

Know your network devices

Beginner level
▣

PC / Host

A device that sends and receives data, such as a laptop or desktop.

⇄

Switch

Connects devices in a local network and forwards frames toward the destination device.

⌘

Router

Connects different IP networks and forwards packets between them.

⟷

Link

A connection between two interfaces. This lab assumes each drawn link is working.

Tip: A switch alone usually connects devices within the same LAN. A router is used when traffic must travel between different networks.
