04 / KEY CONCEPTS

What to notice

01
Destination IP

Routers use the destination IP address to decide where to forward a packet.

02
Next hop

The next router or gateway that moves the packet closer to its destination.

03
Hop count

Each router crossed is one hop. The source and destination hosts are not routers.
