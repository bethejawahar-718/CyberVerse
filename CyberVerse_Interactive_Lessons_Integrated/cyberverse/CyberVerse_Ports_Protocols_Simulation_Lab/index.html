QUICK CONCEPTS

Remember

Port number

A number used by transport protocols to direct data to the right application or service.

TCP

Provides a connection-oriented byte stream with reliability and ordered delivery.

UDP

Sends datagrams without establishing a connection; delivery and ordering are not guaranteed.

Socket

A communication endpoint commonly identified by an IP address, transport protocol, and port.
